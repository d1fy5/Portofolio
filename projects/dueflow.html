03 — DueFlow

DueFlow

Calm, Automated Payment Follow-Ups for Freelancers

A fullstack web application designed and built with React, Vite, and Supabase that organizes unpaid invoices, tracks client follow-up cadences, and automatically prepares courteous, professional reminder messages.

Frontend & UI/UX · React 18 · Vite · Supabase

Web Application

Key Screens & Architecture

Case Study

Problem & Solution

Why chasing payments is stressful and how DueFlow solves it.

Problem

Chasing invoices is awkward and drains focus

Freelancers often postpone sending payment reminders because asking clients for money feels uncomfortable and confrontational. Unpaid invoices get scattered across email threads and spreadsheets, leading to delayed cash flow and mental friction.

Solution

Calm automation with human oversight

DueFlow silently monitors invoice due dates. When a payment approaches or passes due, DueFlow drafts a context-aware, polite email reminder that the freelancer can review, adjust, and send in one click — eliminating anxiety while maintaining strong client relationships.

Core Objectives

Product Goals

Principles guiding the product experience from interface to architecture.

01

Low Cognitive Load

A muted, organic green palette with topographic motifs makes financial review feel calm and peaceful rather than overwhelming.

02

Polite Tone Tiers

Tiered cadences progress gracefully from friendly pre-due heads-ups to clear follow-ups, protecting client trust.

03

Real-time Supabase Backend

Secure user authentication and database models for invoices, client history, and follow-up states with row-level security.

04

Speed & Ergonomics

Built with Vite and React 18, offering instant hot-reloads, responsive layout grids, and accessible interactive components.
